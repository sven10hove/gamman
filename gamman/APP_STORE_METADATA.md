### Description
gamman is a nervous system journal and learning companion. Log your daily state, notice patterns, and explore practical, trauma-informed lessons that build regulation skills over time.

Key features:
- Journal your nervous system state with intensity, triggers, and body sensations
- Generate personalized insights from your entries
- Learn with AI-assisted lessons grounded in polyvagal theory
- Save and revisit lessons for continued practice

Your data stays on your device. AI features are optional and require your own API key.
