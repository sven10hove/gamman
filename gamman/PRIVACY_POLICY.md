## Privacy Policy (Draft)

Last updated: 2026-02-01

### Overview
gamman is a nervous system journal and learning app. Your data stays on your device by default. Optional AI features transmit user-provided content to third-party AI services only when you initiate those features.

### Data we collect
On-device data:
- Journal entries, including text, triggers, and body sensations
- App settings and preferences

Optional data transmitted to third parties (only when you use AI features):
- Prompts or journal summaries you submit for lesson generation or insights
- Section metadata used to generate lesson images or find external resources

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or precise location data.

### How we use data
- Provide journaling and learning features
- Generate AI-assisted lessons and insights when requested

### Sharing
When AI features are used, content is sent to third-party processors:
- Anthropic (text generation)
- OpenAI (image generation)
- Exa (resource search)

We do not sell your data.

### Data retention
- Journal data is stored locally on your device until you delete it.
- We do not maintain a server-side account or database for your content.

### Security
- API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain.
- Network requests use HTTPS.

### Children’s privacy
This app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

### Changes to this policy
We may update this policy as features evolve. The latest version will be available at the privacy policy URL.
